About the Brand Atlas

An interactive reimagining of the supplied “United States of Brands” image. Explore 261 brand profiles across the 48 contiguous U.S. states, even offline.

How to explore

Click a brand label to open its profile. Click a state or “+N” to browse its brands. Switch to “All brands” to explore the full collection. Search by brand name or keywords, and combine your search with industry and state filters. Drag to pan, use the zoom buttons, or hold Ctrl / ⌘ while scrolling to zoom.

How to read the map

State placements reflect historical roots, founding locations, or headquarters connections, following associations in the reference image. They do not consistently indicate current headquarters. Each profile separately explains its origins. A founding or origin year may differ from a brand's launch, incorporation, or merger date; the description provides context. Profiles summarize public historical information and do not provide live business or financial data.

To keep the map readable, it shows a selection of brands in each state. Use the state lists or “All brands” to find the rest. The collection includes identifiable brands from the reference image and additional entries; it is not a complete reproduction. Text labels identify brands and are not official logo artwork. Inclusion does not imply endorsement or authorization.

Sources and verification

Each profile links to the brand's official website, a successor organization, or historical information. Use those sources to check current details. State boundaries use US Atlas, based on U.S. Census Bureau boundary data. The reference image is credited to International Relations Illustrated.

The HTML file runs locally without uploading search terms or browsing activity. An internet connection is only needed when following external links.
